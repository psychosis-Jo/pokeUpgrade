Use case: ui-mockup
Create one polished portrait mobile game gameplay-screen concept for the Chinese WeChat game “熟人局升级”. Show the interface itself edge to edge, without a phone frame, marketing poster, or multiple screens.

Art direction: a cozy friends' house party in a Chinese apartment, expressed through restrained soft 3D toy-like characters and a clear, usable mobile card-game UI. A pale wooden coffee table occupies the central play area, with subtle sofa cushions and warm lamplight near the outer edges. Warm and playful, suitable for adult friends. Avoid casino imagery, green felt, chips, money, gold luxury, neon, or excessive scenery.

Layout: tall portrait screen. Compact title “熟人局升级” at the top, then a compact status strip “打 2 · 红桃主” and “闲家得分 35 / 80”. Four players sit in a cross around the coffee table: teammate at top, opponents left and right, self at bottom. Each opponent has a small charming 3D avatar, short Chinese nickname, and remaining-card count. Label the top player “队友”. Label the bottom player's small portrait “你 · 庄家”. One opponent has playful hand-drawn cat whiskers and a tiny flower on their cheeks, visibly part of an avatar interaction effect. No extra floating punishment controls during the active hand.

Middle: clearly separate each player's played cards spatially near their seat; an example completed four-player trick with one white playing card per player, showing heart A, heart K, heart 10, and heart 5 respectively. Subtle winner outline, a short central message “队友赢墩 · 本墩 15 分”. No duplicate card lists or spreadsheet-like rows.

Bottom third: prioritize a realistic 25-card Tractor hand with two decks, displayed as two readable staggered rows of 13 and 12 cards. Consistent red hearts and diamonds, dark spades and clubs, large crisp corner ranks and suit symbols on warm-white cards. A small unobtrusive “主” tab identifies trump cards. One selected card rises slightly with a blue outline. Keep hand cards above the controls, no overlap with avatars or buttons. Show “已选 1 张”. Bottom buttons, generously sized: “清空”, “推荐”, and an emphasized orange “出牌”. Leave safe-area padding beneath.

Palette: oat white #F5EBDD, pale wood #D5B48C, warm apricot orange #F3A34B for primary actions, lake blue #367E95 for teammate and selected states, berry red #B83249 for red suits, charcoal #283238 for text and black suits. Rounded Chinese sans-serif headings, highly legible utility text. Clear hierarchy, restrained decoration, practical mobile touch targets. The memorable element is the expressive friends around a coffee table; cards remain the clearest element. Chinese labels should be correctly spelled and readable. No English labels, watermark, or invented currency.
